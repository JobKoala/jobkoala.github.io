4.1What Operations Do in the Console
OPERATIONS  /  FOUR THINGS OPERATIONS DO HERE
What the operations team actually does in the console, in the order it comes up — put a job up, fix a candidate's details, set the contact users see, and keep every global list current.

Put a job up for an employer

Create, edit or take down any posting

Operations can publish and edit jobs on an employer's behalf — same 18 fields as the app form, plus two the app does not have: Operation Views and Is Top.

IN THE CONSOLEJobs · Job List

Fix a candidate's details and resume

Edit what the employer will see

Open any account or resume from the console and correct it — wrong region, broken salary range, a resume that will not display. Changes show in the app immediately.

IN THE CONSOLEJobs · Resume Management · Users

Set the contact details users see

One number, one address, everywhere

The service phone, email and hours that both apps print under Contact Us are typed here — change them once and both sides pick it up without a release.

IN THE CONSOLESystem · Basic Info · Content

Everything else that is global

Lists, banners, plans, templates

Regions, education types, industries, home banners, promotion plans, FAQ and notification wording — the pickers and the copy that every screen in both apps reads from.
