Education Level Management

Resumes, job education requirements and admin filters use the same education options.

Do not rename, disable or delete configured education levels arbitrarily

Admin · Education Level List

Bachelor

Identifier: bachelor

Order: 4 · Enabled

One Education Level, Three Uses

Job Seeker Education

Select Bachelor in a resume or profile

to indicate personal education.

Employer Requirements

Select Bachelor as the job education requirement.

Candidates can also be filtered by education.

Resume Filtering

Filter by Bachelor in Resume Management

to find resumes with this qualification.
