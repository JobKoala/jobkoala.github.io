How to Configure Consultation Fees

One Standard Fee for All Advisers

In paid mode, set the product code, currency and amount. Web/Android supports AUD, USD or CNY. Verify both currency and amount.

iOS Product Identifier

The system generates a consumable Product ID from the product code. Apple product IDs are fixed after creation; use a new product code to replace an existing product.

What Each Purchase Unlocks

Example · One Adviser, Two Services

At the example price, AUD 20.00 permanently unlocks an adviser's work visa consultation. Skilled migration consultation with the same adviser costs another AUD 20.00.

Checking Orders After a Price Change

Saved settings apply globally. Existing orders retain the configuration version, currency, amount and product snapshot recorded at creation.
