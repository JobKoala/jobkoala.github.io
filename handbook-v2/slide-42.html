WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW
1
One document, both apps

Job seekers and employers accept the same terms — there is no separate version per side.

2
Accepted once, readable later

A user ticks it at sign-up and cannot finish registering without it. Afterwards the same text sits under My · Settings.

3
Who owns the wording

The business, with legal. It ships with the build, so a change to it is a release, not a console edit.

⚙️

Nothing for operations to do here for now

The text is written once by the business and loaded with the build — operations do not edit it at this stage. The console page stays so the live wording can be checked, and so a lawyer-approved replacement can be dropped in later.
