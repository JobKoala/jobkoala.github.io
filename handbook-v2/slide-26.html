What a role is

A role is a set of permissions, e.g. “Ops Specialist”. A person with that role can open only what it allows.

2
Pick a role

Find the role in the list and click the blue Assign on its row.

3
Tick what it can open

Tick the pages this role may use. Untick the ones it may not.

4
Click Confirm

Everyone with this role gets the change right away — no need to log in again.
