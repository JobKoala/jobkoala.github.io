Key Checks When Revising

Information Collected and Its Uses

The policy covers phone numbers, emails, job preferences and resumes, and explains uses such as job matching and resume enhancement. Check any changes against actual operations.

Effective Date and Last Update

The policy takes effect on 25 Feb 2026; the admin update date is 13 May. Use the effective date in the policy to determine when it applies.

Matching Chinese and English Clauses

Compare the legal entity, information categories, purposes and effective date across both versions.

Do Not Change the Privacy Policy Arbitrarily

For changes to information collected, purposes or effective date, confirm the final wording before updating each language version.
