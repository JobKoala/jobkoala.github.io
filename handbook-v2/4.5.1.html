Job Listing Management

Administrators can maintain published jobs, improve recruitment content and post new jobs on behalf of employers.

Edit Published Jobs

Verify the job ID, then click Edit in its row.

Example: Job 874 · Salon assistant

Clarify duties: help stylists wash and dry hair,

welcome clients and handle bookings.

If pay, hours or requirements change,

confirm with the employer before updating.

Post Jobs via the Admin Panel

Click Add Job above the list to create a job.

Enter the employer's basic details, pay, location

and company information; verify before submitting.
