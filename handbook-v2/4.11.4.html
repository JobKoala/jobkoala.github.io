Key Checks When Revising

Parties and Scope

The agreement shown is between the platform company and individual users of the app. Changing the parties or intended users changes the scope of the terms.

Platform Role and Service Boundaries

The platform facilitates information exchange and matching. Employment contracts are between users and employers. Keep this distinction when updating service descriptions, including job recommendations.

User Obligations in Both Languages

Check requirements for accurate information, account use and prohibited conduct clause by clause. Ensure both languages impose the same obligations.

Do Not Modify the Agreement Arbitrarily

Confirm the final wording before updating the relevant language versions if changes affect the parties, platform responsibilities or user obligations.
