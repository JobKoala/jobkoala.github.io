1
Add a person

Click + Add Administrator and fill in the form. That person can now log in to the console.

2
Decide what they can do

Click Assign Roles on their row, tick one or more roles, click Confirm.

3
Stop them logging in

Click Disable. They can't log in, but nothing is lost — enable them again any time.

4
Remove them for good

Click Delete. The account is gone. Not sure? Use Disable instead.

TipSomeone forgot their password? Click Reset Password on their row.
