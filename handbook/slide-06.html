1
Tap the company name

On a job card the company name is the only part that leaves the job — everything else keeps you on it. The company card inside the job detail opens the very same page (2.4), so there is no “company” tab to go looking for.

2
Or the company card in the job detail

Scroll a job post and the employer’s card sits in the middle of it — name, industry, headcount and the map. Tapping it lands on the very same page, so both routes end up here. See 2.4.

3
Read who they are

Founded year, headcount, industry, logo and a Visa Sponsorship badge when they sponsor, then the profile blurb, the address on a map and photos of the workplace. Tap the map and your own maps app opens with directions — the one thing here that is not read-only; the employer maintains the rest on their side (see 3.9).

4
Pick another of their jobs

Open Positions (4) ends the page: every other role that employer has open, each card opening its own Job Detail. This is the only place in the app where jobs are grouped by employer.
